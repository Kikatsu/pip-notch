Pip 0.1.0 (beta)
A notch companion that shows what your AI coding agents are doing.

Needs: macOS 14 (Sonoma) or later. Works on Apple Silicon and Intel Macs.

INSTALL
1. Drag Pip.app into your Applications folder.
2. Double-click it. macOS will say Pip "can't be opened" because it isn't
   signed by an Apple-registered developer yet. Click Done (or OK).
3. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
   "Open Anyway" next to the message about Pip. Confirm with your password.
4. Pip now opens normally. Look for the eyes in your menu bar and notch.

OR, from Terminal (one command, same result):
   xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Pip.app

FIRST RUN
- Pip connects to the AI coding agents it finds on your Mac (Claude Code,
  Codex, Cursor and others) by adding hooks to their config files. It keeps
  a backup of each file as <file>.pip-backup.
- To stop that, open Settings from the eyes in the menu bar and turn off
  "Connect agents automatically", or press Disconnect next to an agent.
- The first time you click a session in iTerm2 or Terminal, macOS asks to
  let Pip control it. Allow it so Pip can jump to the right window.

Try Settings > "Preview All States" to see everything Pip can show.
