# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Pip to be a friendly, welcoming project for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that helps:

- Being kind and patient, especially with newcomers
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, or derogatory comments
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Publishing others' private information without their permission
- Trolling or deliberately derailing discussions
- Any other conduct that would be inappropriate in a professional setting

## Enforcement

The maintainers may remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that don't follow this Code of Conduct, and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour is harmful.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer privately through their [GitHub profile](https://github.com/Kikatsu). All reports will be reviewed promptly and kept confidential.
